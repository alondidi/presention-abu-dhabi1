Abu Dhabi Capital City

A modern city with a strong economy and rich history

Major Topics People • Geography • Business • Religion • Culture
👥 People & Faith

Diversity & Tradition

Population

Over 1.5 million people live in the city, with residents from a lot of different countries.

Religion

Islam is the official religion, shaping the culture and daily life of the city.

Harmony

Religious freedom is respected, allowing people of all faiths to practice safely and peacefully.

📍 Location

An Island City

The Persian Gulf

The city is located on an island in the Persian Gulf, connected to the mainland by major bridges.

🗺️ Main Regions

Three Main Parts

The City

The modern, busy center.

Al Ain

The "Garden City" with oases.

Al Dhafra

The vast desert region.

⚙️ Business & Economy

Beyond Oil and Gas

Natural Energy

The city has a lot of oil and gas, which powers its growth.

New Focus

The government is investing in tourism, finance, and solar energy.

Future Idea

Abu Dhabi wants to have a strong economy that does not only depend on oil.

Historic Al Ain

Visit old forts and oases that show what life was like long ago.

Jebel Hafeet

A tall mountain with amazing views of the desert and city.

Old Markets

You can still find traditional markets and old buildings in the city.

🔚 Conclusion

A City with a Bright Future

Abu Dhabi is a very modern city that remembers its history. It is a leader in business and culture in the Middle East.
