Abu Dhabi Capital City

A modern city with a strong economy and rich history

Major Topics People • Geography • Business • Religion • Culture
👥 Population

A Growing and Diverse City

Total People

Over 1.5 million people live in the city today.

Mix of People

The city has millions of residents from more than 200 different countries.

Growth

Abu Dhabi is experiencing rapid growth in 2025, largely driven by a booming non-oil economy, significant infrastructure investment, and record population increase.

📍 Geography

An Island City by the Sea

The Location

The city island is in the Persian Gulf. Large bridges connect it to the land.

Main Parts

  • The City: The busy center
  • Al Ain: Beautiful oases
  • Al Dhafra: Desert areas

Travel

Many highways and ports connect Abu Dhabi to nearby cities.

⚙️ Business & Economy

Beyond Oil and Gas

Natural Energy

The city has a lot of oil and gas, which powers its growth.

New Focus

The government is investing in tourism, finance, and solar energy.

Future Idea

Abu Dhabi wants to have a strong economy that does not only depend on oil.

☪️ Religion

Islam and Many Other Faiths

Main Religion

Islam is the official religion and a big part of daily life.

Religious Freedom

People from all over the world can practice their religions freely here.

Respect

The city is a place where different cultures and faiths live in peace.

Art, Culture, and Fun

Grand Mosque

📜 History

From the Desert to the City

Historic Al Ain

Visit old forts and oases that show what life was like long ago.

Jebel Hafeet

A tall mountain with amazing views of the desert and city.

Old Markets

You can still find traditional markets and old buildings in the city.

🔚 Conclusion

A City with a Bright Future

Abu Dhabi is a very modern city that remembers its history. It is a leader in business and culture in the Middle East.
